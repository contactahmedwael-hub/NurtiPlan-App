### Short Punchy Description

**NutriPlan** is a modern web-based food, nutrition, and fitness planner designed to help you discover healthy recipes, scan packaged products using Open Food Facts and TheMealDB, and track your daily wellness journey with interactive summaries.

---

# NutriPlan 🥗

A fully-featured **Food, Nutrition & Fitness Planner** web application built with HTML, Tailwind CSS, and modular JavaScript. NutriPlan empowers users to explore global recipes, scan packaged goods, and keep a detailed daily and weekly food log to achieve their health goals.

---

## Features ✨

* **Meals & Recipes Explorer**: Search and filter global recipes by name, ingredients, categories, and cuisine types (powered by TheMealDB)[cite: 7, 8].
* **Interactive Recipe Details**: View dynamic ingredient checklists, step-by-step instructions, video tutorials, and detailed nutritional facts per serving[cite: 7].
* **Product Scanner & Search**: Look up packaged food items via keywords or barcode numbers to analyze **Nutri-Score** and **NOVA** classifications[cite: 7].
* **Daily Food Log & Progress Tracking**: Keep tabs on your daily calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats with visual progress bars[cite: 7].
* **Weekly Overview & Analytics**: Review your weekly average calorie intake, total logged items, and consistency metrics[cite: 7].
